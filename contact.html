Driveway Parking Guidelines

Our Mercedes Sprinter is 100% self-contained with onboard purified water and battery power. We do not require any water or electrical cords from your house!

✓ Driveway Space: Approximately 24 feet of flat driveway or parking pad.
✓ Height Clearance: 10 feet of overhead clearance (clear of low-hanging tree branches).
✓ Curbside Option: Safe, legal street curb parking in front of your home is also welcome!
