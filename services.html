UNCOMPROMISING STANDARDS

Our Comfort, Safety & Hygiene Ritual

How our self-contained Mercedes Sprinter mobile salon eliminates traditional salon stress, cage drying, and cross-contamination.

1-on-1 Cage-Free Care

Your pet is never put in a cage, cage-bank, or left unattended. They enjoy 100% undivided attention from a certified groomer from greeting to handover.

Medical-Grade Sanitization

Stainless steel tub, hydraulic table, clipper blades, and air systems are disinfected with hospital-grade Virkon spray and UV-C sterilization between every pet.

Whisper Lithium Power

Equipped with high-capacity silent lithium solar battery banks and whisper pumps. No smelly, loud external generators to frighten sensitive pet ears.

Species-Specific Handling

Feline sessions are booked during dedicated quiet hours with lavender aromatherapy. Low-angle hydraulic ramps accommodate senior pets with arthritis.
